Interpretation: once protein length is controlled and models are considered
separately, the MHC-I and MHC-II epitope burdens are near-independent. The two
objectives therefore act on largely disjoint sequence features, and the MHC-II
reduction is NOT a by-product of the MHC-I objective (or vice versa). Note this
bounds, but does not replace, the MHC-II-only ablation arm (roadmap P2.2):
near-zero coupling means an MHC-I-only objective could not have produced the
observed 39% MHC-II drop, but the exact attribution still needs the ablation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Comparison to Gasser et al. 2025 -- READ BEFORE CITING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gasser reports 'relative visibility' = sequence visibility / NATIVE TEMPLATE
visibility, and reaches up to ~70% reduction along a quality/visibility Pareto
curve. Our reduction is measured against BASE-MODEL DESIGNS, not the native
template. The denominators differ, so the numbers are NOT directly comparable.
A like-for-like comparison requires re-scoring the native template sequences.
