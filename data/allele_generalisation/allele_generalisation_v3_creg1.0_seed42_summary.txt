INTERPRETATION  (retention = reduction / trained-DRB1 reference)
  trained DRB1 (reference)   :   29%
  held-out DRB1           :   20%  ( 71% of ref, significant)  -> transfers substantially (partial attenuation)
  DRB3/4/5                :   25%  ( 89% of ref, significant)  -> transfers fully
  HLA-DQ                  :   25%  ( 88% of ref, significant)  -> transfers fully
  HLA-DP                  :   -3%  (-11% of ref, n.s.)  -> does NOT transfer

  Bottom line: the reduction generalises across the DR loci (unseen DRB1 and the
  DRB3/4/5 paralogs) and even to DQ, so the model learned broadly-applicable groove
  chemistry rather than six memorised motifs -- most of the MHC-II repertoire is
  covered without retraining. DP is the exception (no transfer); if DP coverage
  matters it must be trained explicitly (distinct heterodimer groove, weaker predictor).
