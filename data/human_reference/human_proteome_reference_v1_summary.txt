LIMITS ON WHAT THIS CAN SUPPORT
  1. Human proteins are tolerated by thymic negative selection and peripheral
     tolerance, not by presenting few peptides. No predictor used here
     represents that mechanism, so this is a reference SCALE, not a target.
  2. Two unrelated populations: composition, fold class and origin differ at
     once. Length is controlled by the length_matched stratum; nothing else is.
  3. The designs are scored as k=5 sequences per backbone averaged to a
     per-protein mean; each human protein contributes one natural sequence.
     The human spread is therefore between-protein variance only, while the
     design spread has had within-backbone variance averaged out of it.
  4. SwissProt canonical sequences include signal and propeptides that are not
     present in the mature circulating protein, unlike the biologics reference
     set, which was sliced to mature chains.
