READING (fixed before the numbers were computed):
  De-immunised designs falling INTO the biologics range -> the reduction reaches a
    level that proteins already dosed in humans occupy, and the paper can say so
    with this as support.
  De-immunised designs still ABOVE every biologic -> the reduction is real but
    the level is not yet comparable to an approved product. That is the honest
    framing, and it makes the reduction a step rather than an arrival.
  Base designs already inside the range -> the starting point was not unusually
    epitope-rich, and the reduction should not be sold as fixing a pathology.

  OBSERVED: de-immunised median 0.124 lies within the biologics range (0.118-0.253),
            above 17% of them; base median 0.141 lies above 25%.

CAVEATS THAT LIMIT WHAT THIS CAN SUPPORT.
  1. Two unrelated populations. Natural therapeutic proteins and ProteinMPNN
     designs differ in composition, length, fold class and origin at once, so no
     difference here is attributable to any one cause. Reference scale, not a
     controlled comparison. The controlled comparison is base-vs-fine-tuned on
     identical backbones, reported elsewhere in this work.
  2. Predicted presentation is not immunogenicity. Approved biologics with low
     predicted burden can still raise anti-drug antibodies, and human-sequence
     products benefit from central tolerance that no predictor here represents --
     which is precisely why they can be dosed despite the burden they show.
  3. Twelve products is a small, hand-picked set spanning a range, not a sample
     of approved biologics. Quartiles of this set are not population quantiles.
  4. ProPred, not netMHCIIpan. This sits on the conservative instrument of the
     two (P2-1). Run the FASTA through netMHCIIpan to place the reference point
     on the manuscript's primary axis.
