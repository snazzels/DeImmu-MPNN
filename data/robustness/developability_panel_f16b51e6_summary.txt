READING:
  ** 6 metric(s) moved toward HIGHER liability: n_glyc_sequon, deamid_NG, deamid_NS_NT, cysteine, methionine, tryptophan.
     This is the direction that could offset the epitope reduction and must be
     reported as such, with effect sizes, not only as a p-value.
  4 metric(s) moved toward LOWER liability: isomer_DG_DS, hydrophobic_patches, hydrophobic_patches_per100, max_patch_hydropathy.

  A null here is a bounded negative result, not proof of equivalence: with this
  sample a small shift would not be detected, and the proxies are crude by
  construction. What it excludes is a large shift on these axes.
