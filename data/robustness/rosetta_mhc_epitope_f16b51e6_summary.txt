READING (fixed before the numbers were computed):
  ProPred reduction of the same order as netMHCIIpan's -> the reduction is a
    property of the designed sequences, not of netMHCIIpan. The shared-predictor
    objection is answered and the headline can be stated as a reduction in
    predicted presentation without qualifying it to one predictor.
  ProPred reduction clearly smaller but still substantial and significant ->
    partly a real sequence property and partly predictor-specific. Report both
    numbers; the honest claim is the smaller one.
  ProPred reduction near zero or negative -> the model has learned netMHCIIpan's
    idiosyncrasies. The headline would then have to be restated as a reduction
    in netMHCIIpan-predicted presentation specifically, and this becomes the
    paper's principal limitation rather than a supporting panel.

  OBSERVED (matched set, n=98): ProPred windows 11.93% vs netMHCIIpan 41.36%,
            a difference of -29.43 pp.
  -> Materially smaller on the independent predictor. Report both; lead with
     the ProPred figure as the conservative one.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW. ProPred covers eight HLA-DR alleles that only partly
overlap the six-allele DRB1 panel the model was trained against, and scores
9-mer cores rather than 15-mer windows. Agreement therefore supports the
sequence-level claim without extending it to alleles neither predictor covers,
and neither predictor is evidence about T-cell responses in a person.
